Haitian Cuisine Heritage

Site Plan

Site Name

Haitian Cuisine Heritage

This name was selected because it accurately represents the website's mission to preserve and celebrate traditional Haitian culinary traditions. The word "Heritage" emphasizes the cultural and historical significance of the cuisine, while being clear and memorable for visitors seeking authentic Haitian food information.

Optional domain: haitian-cuisine-heritage.com or haitiancuisine.org

Site Purpose

Haitian Cuisine Heritage serves as a comprehensive digital resource dedicated to preserving and sharing authentic Haitian culinary traditions. The website provides:

  • A curated collection of traditional Haitian recipes with detailed instructions and cultural context
  • Information about key ingredients used in Haitian cooking and where to find them
  • Historical background on iconic dishes and their significance in Haitian culture
  • Exploration of different categories including street food, holiday specialties, and everyday meals
  • Educational content about the African, French, and Taíno influences on Haitian cuisine
  • A contact form for visitors to submit family recipes or share their own culinary stories

The site aims to make Haitian cuisine accessible to both the Haitian diaspora seeking connection to their roots and food enthusiasts interested in exploring Caribbean culinary traditions.

Scenarios

The following scenarios represent questions from the target audience that will guide content development:

Scenario 1

Question: "What are the essential ingredients I need to cook authentic Haitian food, and where can I find them?"

Answer: The site will feature a dedicated ingredients page listing staples like epis (Haitian seasoning base), pikliz (spicy pickled vegetables), scotch bonnet peppers, and specific recommendations for sourcing these items both online and in local Caribbean markets.

Scenario 2

Question: "I want to learn how to make griot (fried pork) like my grandmother used to make. Can I find an authentic recipe with step-by-step instructions?"

Answer: The recipes section will include detailed, authentic recipes for traditional dishes like griot, complete with preparation steps, cooking times, cultural context, and tips for achieving the perfect crispy texture.

Scenario 3

Question: "What traditional dishes are typically served during Haitian Independence Day celebrations?"

Answer: The holiday specialties category will highlight ceremonial dishes like soup joumou (pumpkin soup), explaining their historical significance and providing authentic recipes for special occasions.

Color Scheme

The color palette draws inspiration from the vibrant colors of Haitian culture, cuisine, and the national flag:

Haitian Red

#D62828

Usage: Primary headings, call-to-action buttons, navigation highlights, and accent elements. Represents the boldness and passion of Haitian culture.

Deep Blue

#003049

Usage: Secondary headings, navigation bar background, footer background. Provides stability and represents the Caribbean Sea.

Sunset Orange

#F77F00

Usage: Accent color for buttons, links on hover, and highlighting important information. Represents warmth and the vibrant flavors of Haitian cuisine.

Golden Yellow

#FCBF49

Usage: Highlights, borders, and decorative elements. Adds brightness and energy to the design.

Cream

#EAE2B7

Usage: Background color for content sections, card backgrounds. Provides a warm, neutral base that doesn't compete with food photography.

Charcoal

#2C2C2C

Usage: Body text, ensuring excellent readability against light backgrounds.

Typography

Playfair Display

Font Family: 'Playfair Display', serif

Usage: Main headings (h1, h2) and site logo. This elegant serif font adds sophistication and cultural richness, reflecting the heritage aspect of the site.

Sample: Traditional Haitian Recipes

Montserrat

Font Family: 'Montserrat', sans-serif

Usage: Subheadings (h3, h4, h5), navigation links, and button text. This modern, clean sans-serif provides excellent readability and contemporary appeal.

Sample: Ingredients & Preparation

Open Sans

Font Family: 'Open Sans', sans-serif

Usage: Body text, paragraphs, recipe instructions, and general content. Highly legible and comfortable for extended reading.

Sample: Griot is a beloved Haitian dish featuring marinated pork that is boiled until tender, then fried to achieve a perfectly crispy exterior while maintaining juicy, flavorful meat inside. Traditionally served with pikliz and fried plantains, this dish represents the heart of Haitian cuisine.
